## Specialist team

For larger questions AI Marketer consults a team of specialists before it answers. The specialists research in parallel and only read data; AI Marketer then writes the answer and takes any actions (with the usual [confirmations](/ai-marketer/capabilities-and-approvals)).

The team is used when:

- Effort is **Medium** or **High** (never on Low),
- the message is at least 25 characters, and
- the chat is not onboarding or adding a new site.

While it runs, the chat shows a chip per specialist with the depth of their research: *quick check*, *standard* or *deep dive* (for example *Head of SEO · deep dive*). The whole team has a time budget of about 75 seconds.

Specialists:

- Head of SEO
- Head of AI Search (GEO)
- Content Director
- Head of Paid Media
- Technical & CRO Lead
- Competitive Intelligence Lead
- Growth Analyst
- Head of AI Commerce
- Head of Earned Media & Community
- Brand & Positioning Strategist
- Head of Social & Culture
- Chief Data Officer, with Data Operations, Data Quality and Data Governance leads

## Team plans

<Note>Team plans are switched on per workspace.</Note>

For multi-step work AI Marketer can propose a **team plan**: a list of steps assigned to specialists. The plan card shows *Team plan · status · X of Y done*, the revision number and how many experts reviewed it.

- Plan: **Approve and start** (or **Approve changes** for a revision), **Pause**, **Resume**, **Cancel plan**.
- Each step: **Edit**, **Reassign**, **Remove**, **Retry**, **Approve**, **Reject**.

Nothing in a plan starts until a person approves it. When a plan needs you, Work & goals shows *Your team needs a decision to continue.*

## Agents

`/ai/agents` has three tabs: **Agents**, **Activity** and **Skills**.

**New agent** opens the builder: describe what the agent should do, when it runs, and its effort (default Medium). Scheduled agents follow the same confirmation rules as chat.

Triggers *Visibility drop* and *New citation* are **Not available yet**.
