**Monitor** (`/monitor`) shows how AI assistants and search engines answer the questions (prompts) your customers ask, and where your brand appears in those answers.

## Tabs

Visibility, Prompts, Query Fanouts, Platforms, Personas, Sentiment, FactCheck, Citations, Mentions, Gaps and Competitors.

- **Visibility**: Visibility Score and Share of Voice with your rank against competitors. **Download CSV** exports the data.
- **Prompts**: every tracked prompt and how each engine answered it.
- **Query Fanouts**: the follow-up searches AI engines run behind a prompt.
- **Platforms**: results per engine.
- **Personas**: results per buyer persona.
- **Sentiment**: how answers describe your brand.
- **FactCheck**: claims about your brand that may be wrong.
- **Citations**: which pages AI answers cite.
- **Mentions**: where your brand is mentioned.
- **Gaps**: prompts where competitors appear and you don't.
- **Competitors**: competitor visibility. Competitor pages and prices are checked daily, their keywords weekly.

## Metrics

**Visibility Score** for an answer depends on your position in it: 100 at first place, falling with each position (100 × e<sup>−0.35 × (position − 1)</sup>), and 0 if you are not mentioned or below 10th.

**Share of Voice** = your mentions ÷ all brand mentions × 100.

## Adding prompts

**New prompt** (`/prompts/new`) is a three-step wizard: **Prompt**, **Persona**, **Review**. Set a geographic target if answers differ by country; it defaults to Global.

Other ways to add prompts:

- **Discover**: prompts suggested from your site, products and search data.
- **Generate from products**: prompts built from your catalogue.
- **Volumes**: estimated search demand for prompts.
- Ask AI Marketer: *Add prompts for …*.
- Naridon also suggests new prompts weekly from mentions it finds.

Limits depend on your plan. During onboarding and trials you can track 5 prompts and 3 competitors. When you hit the limit: *The plan's prompt limit is reached. Pause or remove a prompt first.*

## Engines

Prompts are run on Google AI Overview and Google Search, ChatGPT Search and Perplexity. Gemini, Copilot and Claude can be added depending on your plan.

## How often prompts run

Naridon runs prompts on a schedule that adapts to how much the answers change:

- Each prompt has its own pace between daily and every 28 days.
- After a change on your site, affected prompts are re-run 1, 2, 3, 7 and 14 days later to measure the effect.
- Prompts whose answers never change are checked every 30 days.
- **Pinned** prompts run daily.
