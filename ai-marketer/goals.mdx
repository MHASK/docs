**Goals** (`/ai/goals`) lets you set objectives with measurable key results (OKRs). Key results are tied to metrics Naridon already measures, so progress updates itself.

Goals need an active plan or trial (*No active plan or trial. Subscribe to use Goals.*).

## Creating a goal

**New goal** asks for:

- **Objective**: what you want to achieve.
- **Why it matters**
- **Period**: Year, Quarter (default), Month, Week or Day. The longest period available follows your billing interval.
- **Part of**: a parent goal, if this one supports it.
- **Key results**: each bound to a metric with a target.

*Baseline is captured from today's value when you create the goal.*

Templates:

- Own AI answers for a topic
- Get cited on the top review and listicle sites
- Grow revenue from AI and organic search
- Beat a competitor on share of voice
- Rank top 10 for a keyword cluster
- Build referring domains

**Suggest goals** proposes goals from your data (1 credit).

A key result whose metric has no data source yet shows *Not measurable yet*. Connect the source (for example GA4 or Search Console) in [Connectors](/ai-marketer/connectors).

## Planning with AI

**Plan with AI** (1 credit) proposes initiatives to reach the goal, *ranked by lift × confidence ÷ effort. Nothing is saved until you accept.* Accepted initiatives become tasks of these kinds: Fix, Content, Outreach, Ad, Technical, Manual.

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **On track** | At least 90% of where linear progress should be today |
| **At risk** | 70–90% |
| **Off track** | Below 70% |
| **Done** | Target reached |
| **No data** | The metric has no value yet |

New goals have a 7-day grace period before they can be at risk or off track.

## Check-ins

Key results are recomputed daily. Every Monday Naridon writes a check-in for each goal from the measured numbers. A goal that is off track two weeks in a row is flagged for re-planning.
