**Work & goals** (`/ai/work`) is where you see what Naridon needs from you and what it has done. The section has tabs **Overview**, **Goals**, **Projects** and **Calendar** (Calendar is switched on per workspace). Managed-service workspaces see *Engagements* instead of Projects.

## Overview

The overview has three tiles.

### Decisions

*Waiting for your approval.* A count of everything that needs a person:

- Fixes waiting to be applied
- Content drafts in review
- Ad batches
- Outreach pitches
- Actions AI Marketer asked to take in chat
- Social reply drafts
- Ad responder replies
- Social posts

**Review decisions** opens [Approvals](/ai-marketer/approvals).

### In progress

*Plans, projects and programmes.* Team plans, projects and running programmes with their progress. **View all work** lists them.

### New results

*Updates you haven't reviewed.* Results from agents and scheduled work since you last looked. **View all activity** opens the activity tab of Agents. What you have already seen is remembered per browser.

## Also on the page

- **Messages from AI Marketer**: proactive notes. **Read messages** opens them.
- *Your team needs a decision to continue.* appears when a [team plan](/ai-marketer/specialist-team#team-plans) is blocked on you. **Open plan** takes you there.
- **Recent activity**: the five newest events. Empty state: *Your work will appear here.*
- **Creative resources**: brand assets and creative material.
