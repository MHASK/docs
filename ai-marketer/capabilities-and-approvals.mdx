AI Marketer acts through **capabilities**: named actions such as *create a prompt*, *apply a fix*, *publish a draft* or *pause a campaign*. Each capability has a risk label, and the label decides whether it runs straight away or asks you.

## Risk labels

| Label | Examples | Runs without asking? |
|---|---|---|
| **Read only** | Look up visibility, list fixes, read analytics | Always runs |
| **Draft** | Write a content draft, draft an ad | Always runs (nothing goes live) |
| **Makes changes** | Apply a fix, change a setting, create prompts | Depends on your [autonomy mode](/ai-marketer/approvals#autonomy-modes) |
| **Outward** | Send an email, post a reply, publish to your site | Always asks |
| **Spends** | Enable a campaign, change a budget | Always asks |
| **Deletes** | Delete a page, remove data | Always asks |

For **Makes changes**:

| Autonomy mode | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Watch | Always asks |
| Assist | Runs low and medium risk changes, asks for high and critical |
| Autopilot | Runs everything except critical changes |

Anything that publishes live is always confirmed.

## Confirmation cards

When AI Marketer needs your approval it shows an **Approval needed** card with the risk label and a preview of exactly what will happen.

- **Approve** runs that action once, with the inputs shown. AI Marketer cannot change them after you approve.
- **Reject** cancels it. The chat says *Nothing was changed.*
- **Permissions** opens the AI permissions settings for that capability.

Cards expire after 15 minutes (*Expired. Ask again to redo it.*).

Some actions can only be done by a person and are never run from chat, even with approval: approving items in the Approvals queue on your behalf, changing AI permissions, inviting team members, approving a team plan and sending outreach test emails.

## AI permissions

**Settings › AI permissions** (`/settings/ai-permissions`) lists every capability by area: Visibility, Prompts, Optimization, Approvals, Autopilot, Competitors, Analytics, Keywords, Goals, Outreach, Content, Site, Search Console, Integrations, Workspace, Agents & schedules, Reports, Brand, Credits & plan, Knowledge and Navigation.

For each capability you can set:

- **Default**: follow the rules above.
- **Always ask**: confirm every time, even if autonomy would let it run.
- **Never**: AI Marketer may not use it.

Overrides can only make things stricter. Changing them needs the Admin or Owner role.
