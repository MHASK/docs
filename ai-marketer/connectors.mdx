The more Naridon can see, the better its answers and the more key results it can measure. Connections live in three places under **Settings › Connections**:

| Page | Path | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| Integrations | `/integrations` | Shop platforms, CMS and other apps |
| Search engines | `/settings/integrations` | Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, sitemaps, IndexNow, Google Analytics 4 |
| Connectors | `/ai/connectors` | Accounts Naridon reads from (analytics, social, ads, email, CRM) |

<Note>The Connectors page is switched on per workspace. *Accounts Naridon reads from. Email content is saved as a draft, never sent.*</Note>

The **What Naridon can see** widget shows your data coverage as a percentage and suggests the next source to connect.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not available yet | Not offered on your account yet |
| Not connected | Connect it |
| Reconnect needed | Access expired or was revoked |
| Waiting for first sync | Connected, data arriving soon |
| Synced | Up to date |
| Out of date | The last successful sync is older than expected |
| Last sync failed | Naridon will retry; reconnect if it keeps failing |

Connectors sync on their own schedule (checked hourly). The first sync of most connectors back-fills 90 days.

**Disconnect**: *Naridon stops reading from this account and revokes its access. Data already synced stays in your reports.*

For connectors that use a key: *Use a read-only key. It is stored encrypted and never shown again.*

## Google Analytics 4

1. **Settings › Search engines**, GA4 card: sign in with Google (read-only analytics access).
2. Choose the GA4 property.

Syncs daily, and **Sync now** pulls the latest. GA4 data feeds [Attribution](/ai-marketer/attribution) and goal metrics.

## Microsoft Clarity

1. In Clarity: *your project → Settings → Data Export → Generate new API token.*
2. Paste the token in Connectors.

Clarity's API only returns recent days, so Naridon syncs one day at a time daily and cannot back-fill history.

## Google Search Console

Sign in with Google and choose the property. Naridon reads search performance and can submit sitemaps.

## Bing Webmaster Tools

Sign in with Microsoft. One click; Naridon reads your Bing sites. You can also turn on IndexNow so changed pages are submitted to Bing and other IndexNow engines.

## Meta (Facebook Pages and Instagram)

Connect with Meta sign-in to read Page and Instagram insights. This is separate from the Meta **ad account**, which you connect in [Ads Studio](/ai-marketer/ads). [Ad responders](/ai-marketer/ad-responders) needs the Page connection.

## Microsoft Advertising

Sign in with Microsoft. Naridon finds up to 10 ad accounts you have access to. The same account can also be managed from [Ads Studio](/ai-marketer/ads).

## CRM

HubSpot and Pipedrive can receive leads from [Ad responders](/ai-marketer/ad-responders) and provide deal revenue for attribution.
