**Approvals** (`/ai/approvals`) is the single queue for every change Naridon proposes. Open it from **Review decisions** on [Work & goals](/ai-marketer/work-home). Older links to `/fixes` and `/optimization/fixes` redirect here.

## The queue

Switch between **Waiting** (needs a decision) and **Shipped** (already live).

Filter by type: **All**, **Fixes**, **Content**, **Ads**, **Outreach**, **Assistant actions** (actions AI Marketer asked to take in chat). Use *Search changes* to find a specific item.

Columns: Change, Type, Source, Priority, Impact, Proposed (or Shipped) date.

### Fixes

Fixes are changes to product or page fields on your site: titles, meta descriptions, alt text, structured data, FAQs, descriptions and similar.

- **Apply** pushes one fix to your site (*Applied to your site*). Select several rows to **Apply N fixes** at once.
- Click a row to open it in AI Marketer with *Walk me through the fix …*, where you can ask questions or ask for a different version.
- In **Shipped**, **Revert** puts the original value back.

The fix detail page (`/optimization/fixes/<id>`) has **Edit**, **Regenerate** and **Undo**. Undo restores the field on your store to its original value.

### Why a fix was proposed

Every fix carries a short explanation (one or two sentences) of what it improves and why it matters for search and AI answers. Fixes created by a fixed rule rather than by AI say *Fix generated by rule …* instead.

### Content

Content drafts show **Publish** and **Reject**. The drawer shows the target, risk, proposed text and a **Publish to** picker for the destination. Rejecting asks *Why?*; the reason is used to improve later drafts. See [Content engine](/ai-marketer/content-engine).

### Ads, outreach and assistant actions

Ad batches, outreach pitches and chat actions are approved or rejected the same way. Approving an ad batch never enables spend on its own; see [Ads](/ai-marketer/ads).

## Autonomy modes

**Autonomy settings** (in the Approvals header, or **Settings › AI Autonomy Level**) decides what is applied without a person.

| Mode | Label | What is applied automatically |
|---|---|---|
| **Watch** | Review everything | Nothing. Every change waits in Approvals. |
| **Assist** | Apply low risk | Low-risk fixes. Medium-risk fixes start applying after 10 applied fixes with no rollbacks. |
| **Autopilot** | Apply all but critical | Everything except critical changes. |

Risk tiers for fixes:

| Tier | Fields |
|---|---|
| Critical | Price, inventory, SKU, vendor, product type, status |
| High | Product description |
| Medium | Titles, meta tags, alt text, structured data, FAQ, URL handle |
| Low | Tags and similar fields |

Other settings on the Autopilot settings page (`/ai/autopilot/settings`):

- **Running / Dry run**: dry run proposes but never applies.
- **Daily limit**: how many changes may be applied per day (3, 5, 10, 25 or 50; default 5).
- **What it may change**: switch individual change types on or off.
- **Guardrails**: *Never change prices*, *Never change inventory*, *Keep brand names intact*, *Keep product types*, *Always ask before changing titles*, *Always ask before rewriting descriptions*.

Automatic applies are also rolled out gradually per workspace, so not every eligible fix is applied the moment it is created.

<Warning>
Content drafts follow their own rules: in Assist and Autopilot some low-risk content types can be published without review. Check the draft type risk table in [Content engine](/ai-marketer/content-engine#draft-types) and use **Dry run** or Watch if every draft must be reviewed.
</Warning>
