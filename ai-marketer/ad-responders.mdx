**Ad responders** (`/ai/ad-responders`) collects everyone who answered your ads in one inbox and drafts replies.

<Note>Ad responders is switched on per workspace.</Note>

Tabs: **Inbox**, **Replies**, **Setup**.

## What is collected

| Source | Facebook | Instagram | LinkedIn |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead form | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Ad comment | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| DM | Yes | Yes | — |

## Requirement: connect the Facebook Page or Instagram account

Meta delivers lead forms, ad comments and DMs to the **Page or Instagram account your ads run from**, not to the ad account. Connecting a Meta ad account in [Ads Studio](/ai-marketer/ads) is not enough.

Connect the Page or Instagram account in **Social**, then in **Setup** press **Subscribe pages** so Naridon receives new responses.

LinkedIn lead forms come from the LinkedIn ad account connected in Ads Studio and are pulled every 10 minutes.

To send leads to your CRM, connect HubSpot or Pipedrive in [Connectors](/ai-marketer/connectors).

## Replies

*Drafted by AI Marketer. Nothing is sent until you approve a reply and press Send.*

Drafts are listed in **Replies** and in [Approvals](/ai-marketer/approvals). Some replies cannot be sent by Naridon:

- DMs older than 24 hours: *The 24-hour window to answer this DM has closed.* (Meta's messaging rule.)
- Lead form follow-ups: *Lead follow-ups are sent from your own email or CRM. Copy the text.*
- LinkedIn comments: copy the text and reply on LinkedIn.
